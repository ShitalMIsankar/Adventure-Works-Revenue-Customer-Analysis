# Adventure Works Sales & Customer Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes **Adventure Works sales and customer data** to understand overall business performance, customer behavior, product performance, regional sales, and time-based trends.

The project uses **Tableau** to transform raw sales data into interactive dashboards and business insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Tableau** – Data visualization, dashboard development, calculated fields
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **Excel** – Source data and data preparation

## 📊 Project Workflow
** Data Cleaning & Transformation → Data Analysis → KPI & Calculated Fields → Tableau Visualization Report → Business Insights**

* Sales Territories
* Date/Time dimensions

## 📌 Key Analysis

### Sales & Profit Analysis

* Analyzed total sales, profit, and profit margin.
* Compared sales and profitability across products and regions.
* Identified top-performing and loss-making products.

### Product Analysis

* Analyzed product and subcategory performance.
* Identified top products by sales and profit.
* Compared product performance across different regions.

### Customer Analysis

* Analyzed customer distribution by country and region.
* Segmented customers based on customer type.
* Identified top customers by sales.
* Performed customer retention analysis.

### Time Series Analysis

* Analyzed yearly sales trends.
* Identified peak sales periods and monthly performance.
* Studied changes in sales and profit over time.
